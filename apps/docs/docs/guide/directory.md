# Directory

**Directory** (in the sidebar, under Contacts) keeps the companies you work
with and the people at them. It's for your suppliers' account executives,
a partner's engineers, the billing contact at your internet provider. The
people who write in to the help desk are a separate list, in
[Contacts](/guide/contacts-and-personal-data).

## Companies

Each company has a type (supplier, customer, partner, internal area or
other), a tax ID (RUT), phone, email, website, address and notes. Click a
company's name to see everything linked to it:
- its people;
- the contracts you have with it;
- the equipment bought from it.

Deleting a company keeps its people in the directory, without a company.
Its contracts and equipment keep the rest of their details.

## People

Each person has a name, job title, area (account executive, management,
sales, technical, support, billing or other), email, phone, mobile and
notes. Only the name is required. Filter the list by area or company, or
search by name, title, company or email.

## Where it's used

- **Contracts**: pick the supplier from the directory, and the person to
  call there. See [Contracts](/guide/cmdb-and-assets#contracts-warranties-and-licenses).
- **Inventory**: record which supplier each piece of equipment was bought
  from. See [Assets](/guide/cmdb-and-assets#assets).

## Who can do what

- **Look people up**: anyone who can read tickets.
- **Add, edit or delete**: roles with *Manage contacts' personal data*,
  which administrators have.

Changes are recorded in the [audit log](/guide/administration), by record
id only, without names.
